TROS Greek Mythology
The Name Behind the Brand

In Greek mythology, Tros was a king of Dardania — a ruler of such honor and dignity that even Zeus took notice. When his son Ganymede was taken to Olympus, Zeus sent Hermes to Tros bearing a gift: two divine horses, swift enough to run across water — a tribute to a king who bore loss with strength. His legacy ran so deep that when his son Ilus built a great city, he named it Troy in his father's honor. A name that echoed for three thousand years.
